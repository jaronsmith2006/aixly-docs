Privacy and EU compliance

Not legal advice

This page is general guidance to help you get started. It isn't legal advice, and other regions have their own rules. Please check your privacy policy with a legal professional.

What Aixly already does for you​

  • AI is always recognizable. The chat shows shoppers "You're chatting with an AI assistant" in their language, as the EU AI Act's transparency rules require. When your team joins, the chat says so.
  • Order details only after verification. Shoppers prove an order is theirs (order number plus email or phone, or being logged in) before seeing it.
  • Retention you control. Choose how long conversations and shared files are kept in Settings → Chat history retention.
  • Data requests handled. Shopify's customer data and deletion requests (GDPR) are processed automatically, and your store's data is removed after you uninstall.
  • Analytics consent. Analytics events follow your store's cookie consent (Shopify Customer Privacy).

What you should do​

  1. Mention the chat in your privacy policy (template below).
  2. Pick a retention period that matches your policy (Settings → Chat history retention).
  3. If you have staff in the chat, make sure they know conversations are stored and visible to your team.

Privacy policy template​

Adapt this to your store and add it to your privacy policy (Settings → Policies in Shopify admin):

Chat assistant. Our website offers a chat, provided by Aixly, where you can ask questions about our products, your orders and our policies. Answers are generated by an AI assistant; members of our team may also reply. We process the messages you send, any files you attach, and contact details you choose to share (such as your email address or phone number) in order to answer your questions, help with your orders and improve our service. If you're logged in, we use your customer account to show your own orders. Chat conversations are stored for [retention period] and then deleted. The AI responses are generated using OpenAI as a sub-processor. You can ask us to access or delete your chat data at any time by contacting us at [your contact email].

Do I need a cookie banner for the chat?​

The chat stores small items in the shopper's browser to keep their conversation across pages, which is needed for the chat to work. Analytics events respect your store's existing cookie consent banner. If you're unsure, ask your legal advisor.
