## Installation & setup

### How do I turn on the chat widget?

Shopify admin → **Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → Aixly AI Chatbot → Save**. The **Setup Guide** on the app's Dashboard links you there.

### Does Aixly change my theme code?

No. It uses a theme app embed, which you can switch off at any time.

### Which languages are supported?

The assistant replies in the language the shopper writes in. The chat interface (buttons, forms) is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese and Chinese, following your store's language.

## The assistant

### Where does it get its answers?

From your live catalog (products, prices, stock), your Shopify policies, your store pages and your knowledge base. It doesn't make up prices, stock or policies.

### It gave a wrong answer. How do I fix it?

Open the conversation in **Chats** and click **Correct this answer** under the reply. Your corrected answer is used from the next message.

### Can shoppers see each other's orders?

No. Order details are only shown after the shopper proves it's their order (order number plus email or phone, or being logged in).

## Live chat

### Can my team chat with shoppers?

Yes. When a shopper asks for a person, your team replies from **Chats** and the assistant pauses. See **Live chat**.

### Do I need to keep the app open?

Your team counts as available while someone has the app open in Shopify admin. You also get email/Slack alerts, including a reminder when a shopper has waited 3 minutes.

### What if the shopper has already left?

If they shared their email, they get your reply by email with a link to continue the same chat.

## Shoppers

### Can shoppers send photos?

Yes: JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 5 MB each and 3 per message. Your team can send files too.

### Can shoppers return items from the chat?

Yes. Verified shoppers choose the items and reason, and a return request appears in Shopify for you to approve.

## Plans & limits

### Why did the widget disappear?

Most often the Starter plan's monthly conversation limit was reached. Check **Plans** in the app.

### Does testing in Settings count toward my limit?

No. **Settings → Try your bot** doesn't count toward your conversations.
