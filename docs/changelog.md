## October 2026 (update 5)

- **Add to cart from the chat**: shoppers ask, the assistant adds the right option and shows View cart and Checkout buttons
- **Ask Aixly**: a support assistant on the Support page that answers from the help center, checks your setup and emails our team when you need a person
- If the assistant can't answer for a moment, shoppers get a form to reach your team instead of an error
- Order lookup only reads the order's email and phone to confirm it's the shopper's

---

## October 2026 (update 4)

- **Online / Away**: click your status on the Chats page to stop offering live chat while you're away from an open tab
- Live chat: if nobody joins within 5 minutes, the assistant takes over again (10 minutes once someone has joined)
- **Chats** in the Shopify admin: the same compact layout as the team inbox, so the conversation gets the space
- Emails from Aixly have a new look, and new stores get a welcome email with the setup steps

---

## October 2026 (update 3)

- **Assigning chats**: give a chat to yourself, a teammate or the store admin; replying claims unowned chats; a **Mine** view; alerts and an email for the assignee
- **Team inbox**: a cleaner layout with more room for the conversation, a profile menu (name, photo, password)
- **28 chat languages**: added Arabic, Czech, Danish, Greek, Finnish, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese; right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew
- Phones: while typing, the chat turns compact so more of the conversation stays visible above the keyboard
- Settings → Team: the invitation link fits the page, with **Copy link** and **New link**
- **Help Center**: search, and screenshots in the articles

## October 2026 (update 2)

- **Team inbox**: invite staff to answer chats with their own sign-in, no Shopify access needed (agent and manager roles)
- **Helpdesk connections**: tickets in Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gorgias, Help Scout or any system via webhook
- **Languages**: choose the chat language (store, browser or fixed), translate every text with AI help, reword all hand-off lines
- Phones: the page behind the chat no longer scrolls or shows through above the keyboard

## October 2026

**Live chat**

- Real-time live chat: messages, typing indicators and team joins appear instantly
- See what a shopper is typing before they send it
- Previews of your latest messages above the chat button when the shopper has closed the chat
- Missed replies are emailed to shoppers with a **Continue the chat** link
- Sound and in-app alerts on any page, plus email/Slack reminders when a shopper waits 3 minutes
- Photos and PDFs in both directions (JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF up to 5 MB)
- Live chat satisfaction ratings

**Inbox**

- **Suggest reply**: an AI draft of your reply that you edit before sending
- Saved replies with the `/` shortcut and `{first_name}`
- Tags and a tag filter
- Orders placed after chatting shown on the conversation

**Smarter assistant**

- Saved answers and **Correct this answer** apply from the very next message
- **Teach the assistant**: answers your team gives in live chats are suggested for your knowledge base
- Returns filed directly in Shopify from the chat

**Sales**

- **Aixly impact** on the Dashboard, with stricter attribution (only shoppers who actually used the chat)
- Cart reminder with an optional discount code

**Storefront**

- **Embedded chat window**: a theme block that puts the chat inside any page section
- Open the chat from your own buttons (`data-aixly-open`) or JavaScript events (`aixly:open`)
- Shoppers always see that they're chatting with an AI assistant

**Dashboard and support**

- "Where shoppers find the chat" overview with copyable links and code
- Chat activity (visitors, chats, AI replies) with trends vs the previous period, and recent chats with automatic tags
- New **Support** page with privacy and EU compliance guidance

**Widget and admin**

- Icon size control and custom launcher icons
- The Appearance preview shows the real widget, including the chat button
- Mobile: full-screen chat without page scrolling behind it
- Simpler installation using Shopify's standard flow (no extra redirects)
