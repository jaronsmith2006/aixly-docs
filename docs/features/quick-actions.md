## What are quick actions?

Quick actions are buttons in the chat, above the message box, that help shoppers start common tasks with one tap.

Examples:

- "Track my order"
- "Size guide"
- "Talk to a person"

On phones they sit in one row that shoppers can swipe; on larger screens they wrap onto extra lines. They're hidden while your team is chatting live.

---

## Where to configure them

- In the app, open **Appearance → Quick actions** (up to 6)

![Appearance → Quick actions](/img/screens/appearance-quick-actions@2x.webp)

---

## Action types

- **Send a message**: posts a message into the chat and the assistant answers. Best for "Return policy", "Show bestsellers", "Talk to a person".
- **Open a link**: opens a page you choose. Best for your account page, contact page or a booking page.
- **Order status form**: shows a secure form for the order number and email/phone, then the order status. Best for "Track my order".
