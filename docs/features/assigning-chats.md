Give each conversation an owner so the right person answers it, and nobody answers twice.

![Assigning a chat to a teammate](/img/screens/assign-menu.webp)

---

## Assign a chat

Open the conversation in **Chats** (or in the [team inbox](./team-inbox.md)) and click **Assign** at the top. Choose:

- **Store admin**: anyone using Aixly in your Shopify admin
- Any **team member** with access to your store (their photo or initials, and their role, are shown)
- **Unassign** to give it back to everyone

Anyone with inbox access can assign, both the store and team members. Reassign a chat any time, for example to a manager who knows your returns policy.

---

## Chats you reply to become yours

When you reply to, or join, a chat that nobody owns, it's assigned to you automatically. A chat that already belongs to a teammate stays theirs.

---

## Finding your chats

- **Mine** (next to **Needs reply**) lists the open chats assigned to you.
- In the list, a chat shows **Yours**, or **→ name** for a teammate's.

---

## Notifications

When a teammate assigns a chat to you:

- you hear the alert sound and see a notification while the inbox is open
- team members also get an email with an **Open the chat** link that goes straight to the conversation

---

## When someone leaves

Pausing or removing a team member (Settings → Team) gives their chats back to everyone, so nothing gets stuck with a person who can't answer.
