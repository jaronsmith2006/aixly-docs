## Where to find it

- In the app, open **Settings → Languages**

---

## Which language does the chat use?

- **The language the shopper is browsing your store in** (recommended): follows your store's languages in Shopify (Markets, Translate & Adapt). A shopper on your French storefront gets the chat in French.
- **The shopper's browser language**: useful if your store is in one language but your shoppers aren't.
- **Always one language**: pick it from the list.

Whatever you choose, the assistant answers in the language the shopper writes in.

Built in for **28 languages**: buttons, forms, the AI notice and every system line ("our team is joining", "you're chatting with the assistant again"…).

| | |
|---|---|
| Europe | English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian |
| Asia & Middle East | Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew |

Arabic and Hebrew chats are laid out right to left.

## Translating your own texts

Set **Your texts are written in** to the language you type your texts in. Then, under **Translations**, click **Translate** next to a language. Languages you've translated are listed first; click **Show all 28 languages** (and use the finder) for the rest.

![Settings → Languages](/img/screens/settings-languages.webp)

- Every text shoppers can see is listed (chat title, greeting, AI notice, live-chat lines, quick actions, popups, cart reminder), with your original under each field.
- **Fill empty fields with AI** drafts the translations for you. Placeholders like `{code}` and `{name}` stay as they are. Review, then **Save translations**, then save the page.
- **Preview** shows the chat in that language.

Empty fields use your original text, except system lines, which use the built-in translation for that language.

---

## Editing the system lines

In **Settings → Live chat & handoff** you can reword:

- Message when the team is joining / offline
- When a team member joins (`{name}` becomes the team member's or your team's name)
- When the chat goes back to the assistant
- When nobody from the team could join

The AI notice under the chat header is in **Appearance → Window**. It's always shown (shoppers must be able to tell they're chatting with an AI), but you can word it your way.
