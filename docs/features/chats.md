## Where to find it

- In the app, open **Chats**. It has two tabs: **Inbox** and **Teach the assistant**.

Everything updates live: new messages, shoppers typing, and takeovers by teammates.

---

## Inbox

### Finding conversations

- **Needs reply**: shoppers waiting for your team, and live chats with unread messages
- **Mine**: open chats assigned to you
- **Live**: chats your team is in right now
- **All** and **Closed**
- **Search** messages, email or name
- **Tags**: filter by a tag you added

Each row shows the latest message (or "Typing…"), the status, who it's assigned to, tags and the shopper's rating.

### In a conversation

- The full conversation, including what the assistant said and the products it showed
- The shopper's contact details (if they shared them), whether they're logged in, and **orders they placed after chatting**
- A summary for your team when a shopper asked for a person
- **Join chat**, **Hand back to AI**, **Assign** (to yourself, a teammate or the store admin; see **Assigning chats**), **Close** / **Reopen**
- **Tags**: add tags such as *refund* or *vip* with **+ Tag** (up to 10)
- **Correct this answer** under any assistant reply (see **Knowledge base and teaching the assistant**)

### Replying

See **Live chat** for typing previews, **Suggest reply**, saved replies and attachments.

---

## Teach the assistant

Questions the assistant couldn't answer and answers your team gave, ready to save to your knowledge base. See **Knowledge base and teaching the assistant**.

---

## Tips

- Keep **Needs reply** empty: those shoppers are waiting.
- Use tags to spot patterns (for example how many chats are about *shipping-delay*).
- Save your most common replies as saved replies.
