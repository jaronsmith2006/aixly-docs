Your team can answer chats from any browser with their own sign-in. They don't need a Shopify account or access to your Shopify admin.

![The team inbox](/img/screens/team-inbox.webp)

---

## Invite your team

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Enter their email, choose a role and click **Send invitation**.
   - **Agent**: answers and assigns chats.
   - **Manager**: also teaches the assistant (Teach the assistant, Correct this answer) and sees unanswered questions.
3. They open the link in the email (valid for 7 days, works once), enter their name and choose a password.

If email isn't set up for your store yet, the invitation link is shown so you can send it yourself with **Copy link**. Lost the email? Click **New link** next to the invitation (the old link stops working).

![Settings → Team](/img/screens/settings-team.webp)

## Signing in

The team inbox is at **app.aixly.io/team**. It's the same live inbox as in the app: typing previews, suggested replies, saved replies, tags, files and alerts.

- The top bar shows the store and your role. People who help several stores switch between them there.
- **Inbox** and **Teach the assistant** (managers) are at the top left; **Sound on/off** at the top right.
- **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page sends a reset link (valid for 1 hour). Resetting signs you out everywhere else.

---

## Your profile

Click your name or photo at the top right.

![Profile menu](/img/screens/team-profile-menu.webp)

- **Edit profile**: your photo and your name (what your teammates see).
- **Change password**: enter your current password and a new one (10+ characters, with a number or symbol). Other devices are signed out; this one stays signed in.
- **Sign out**

![Your profile](/img/screens/team-profile.webp)

Photos can be JPG, PNG or WebP up to 2 MB. They're shown next to chats assigned to you.

---

## Assigning chats

Give each chat an owner. Anyone on the team can assign a chat to a teammate or to the store admin, and replying claims a chat nobody owns. See [Assigning chats](./assigning-chats.md).

---

## Managing access

In **Settings → Team**:

- **Make manager / Make agent** changes the role.
- **Pause** stops access right away; **Restore** gives it back.
- **Remove** takes them off your team (their account stays for other stores they help).

Paused or removed people's chats go back to everyone.

---

## Showing names to shoppers

By default replies are signed with your team name. Turn on **Show each team member's first name to shoppers** in **Settings → Live chat & handoff** to sign replies from the team inbox with the person's first name.

---

## Security

Passwords are stored as strong one-way hashes, repeated wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes, invitation and reset links work once, and access is checked on every request.
