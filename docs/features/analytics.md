## Aixly impact (Dashboard)

The top of your **Dashboard** shows the last 30 days:

- **Sales influenced**: revenue and orders from shoppers who used Aixly before buying (see below)
- **Conversations**
- **Handled by the assistant**: the share of conversations resolved without your team
- **Live chat satisfaction**: the share of 4–5 star ratings, the average and the number of ratings

---

## How sales are attributed

An order counts as **influenced by Aixly** when, within **7 days before** the order, the shopper:

- chatted with the assistant or your team, **or**
- clicked or added a product to their cart from the chat, **or**
- used the cart reminder.

Logged-in customers are matched even if they order on another device. Simply opening the chat window doesn't count. Influenced orders also appear on the shopper's conversation in **Chats**.

## Analytics page

In the app, open **Analytics**:

- Conversations and trends
- Escalations to your team
- Engagement funnel (widget loads → opens → messages → product clicks → add to cart → checkout)
- Influenced orders, revenue and average order value, per day
- Top products shown, clicked, added to cart and purchased
- Usage charges (if your plan has overage billing)

## Using it well

- Many conversations but few product clicks: add quick actions for bestsellers, and make your greeting invite product questions.
- Many escalations about the same topic: answer it once in **Teach the assistant**.
- Low satisfaction: read the comments on low ratings in **Chats**, and set up saved replies for consistent answers.
