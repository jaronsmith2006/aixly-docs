## Where to find it

- In the app, open **Appearance**

![Appearance, with the live preview on the right](/img/screens/appearance@2x.webp)

The preview on the right runs the real storefront widget with your unsaved settings, so what you see is exactly what shoppers get. Click **Save settings** when you're happy. Changes reach your storefront within a couple of minutes.

## Button (chat launcher)

The preview on this tab shows the closed chat button, as shoppers first see it; click it to open the chat.

- **Position**: left or right
- **Shape**: circle or square (corner radius applies to square buttons)
- **Button size**: small, medium or large
- **Content**: icon, icon + text, or text
- **Icon**: choose a preset or **Upload** your own (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF; it's resized and stored safely, SVG isn't accepted)
- **Icon size**: adjusts the icon inside the button, separately from the button size. At the largest size an uploaded image fills the whole button (great for a round logo).
- **Label**: the text for "icon + text" or "text" buttons
- **Show launcher**: hide the button if you open the chat from your own links instead
