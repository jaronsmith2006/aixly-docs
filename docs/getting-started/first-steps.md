## Recommended setup order

### 1) Make the widget yours

- Open **Appearance**
- Set colors, the chat button (shape, size, icon or your own logo), greeting and quick actions
- The preview on the right shows exactly what shoppers will see
- Click **Save settings**

### 2) Teach the assistant your store

- Open **Settings → Knowledge base training**
- Add your FAQ, sizing and care pages, documents (size guides, manuals) and short question-and-answer pairs
- Your published store pages and policies are already read automatically

### 3) Set up live chat

- Open **Settings → Live chat & handoff**
- Choose the team name shoppers see, your business hours, and keywords that should always reach a person (for example *chargeback* or *allergic reaction*)
- In **Settings → Notification settings**, add the email (and optionally a Slack webhook) for alerts when a shopper is waiting

### 4) Choose how shoppers prove an order is theirs

- Open **Settings → Order lookup**
- Require the email, the phone, or either one, together with the order number. Logged-in customers don't need to enter anything.
- Decide whether returns are filed directly in Shopify (**File return requests in Shopify**)

### 5) Test it like a customer

Use **Settings → Try your bot**, or open your storefront in a private window and ask:

- "What's your return policy?"
- "Do you have this in size M?"
- "Where is my order?"
- "Can I talk to a person?" (then reply from **Chats**)

### 6) Keep improving

- Check **Chats → Teach the assistant** every few days and save answers for questions the assistant couldn't handle
- Use **Correct this answer** under any assistant reply in a conversation to fix it from the very next message
