Settings

Where to find it​

  • In the app, open Settings

Try your bot​

Chat with your assistant right inside Settings. It works exactly like the live widget but doesn't count toward your conversation quota, and it never creates real requests or returns.

Order lookup (verification)​

Shoppers must prove an order is theirs before seeing its details. Choose what they enter together with the order number:

  • Email only
  • Phone only
  • Email or phone

Logged-in customers are recognized automatically and can see their recent orders without entering anything. Repeated wrong guesses are blocked.

File return requests in Shopify: when on, a verified shopper picks the items and the reason in the chat, and a return request appears in Shopify → Orders → Returns for you to approve or decline (Shopify then emails them the next steps). When off, returns arrive in Requests like other order requests. See Order requests and returns.

Knowledge base training​

See Knowledge base and teaching the assistant.

Custom routing​

Suggest a specific link when shoppers ask about a topic, for example "Book a test ride" → your booking page.

Tone, response style and personality​

  • Formal or conversational tone
  • Short or detailed answers
  • Humor and emoji use
  • Assistant name
  • Your own instructions (brand voice, things to always mention)

Your instructions shape the tone, but they can't make the assistant invent prices, stock or policies: those always come from your store.

Live chat & handoff​

See Live chat.

Topics to avoid​

Topics the assistant should politely decline (for example competitors or medical advice).

Where the chat appears​

  • Hide on the cart page
  • Hide on these pages: paths starting with /, such as /pages/contact (subpages are included)

Chat history retention​

Keep conversations forever, or delete them automatically after 30 days to 2 years. Shared files are deleted with them.

Notification settings​

  • Notification email and Slack incoming webhook URL for alerts: a shopper asked for a person, a shopper has been waiting for a few minutes, or you're nearing your plan's limit
