Live chat

How shoppers reach your team​

A shopper is handed to your team when they:

  • ask for a person (for example "Can I talk to someone?"), or
  • use one of your escalation keywords (for example chargeback, lawyer, allergic reaction).

The assistant summarizes the conversation for you and tells the shopper someone will join shortly. If your team is offline (outside business hours, or nobody has the app open), the shopper leaves their email or phone instead, and you'll find the request in Chats.

Settings (Settings → Live chat & handoff)​

  • Let shoppers chat with your team: turn live chat on or off
  • Team name shown to shoppers: for example "Store team" or "Ana"
  • Message when the team is joining and Message when the team is offline
  • Escalation keywords: comma-separated
  • Only offer live chat during business hours: set your hours per day and Time zone
  • Email shoppers the replies they missed: see below

Your team counts as available while someone has the app open in Shopify admin (any page) or the team inbox open.

Online, Away, Offline​

The badge next to the inbox tabs shows your status. Click it to switch:

  • Online: shoppers who ask for a person are offered live chat while you have the app open.
  • Away: having the app open doesn't offer shoppers live chat. Use it when you step away and leave the tab open. Teammates who are online can still take chats. Saved per browser.
  • Offline: live chat is off, or it's outside your business hours. Shoppers leave their email or phone instead. Change this in Settings → Live chat & handoff.

Alerts, so nobody waits​

  • In the app: a sound and a notification on any page when a shopper is waiting or writes in a live chat. Turn the sound on or off with Sound alerts on the Chats page (saved per browser).
  • Email / Slack: right away when a shopper asks for a person, and a reminder if a shopper has waited 3 minutes without a reply (once per wait). Set the recipients in Settings → Notification settings.

Chatting​

Open the conversation in Chats and reply. Sending a reply joins the chat and pauses the assistant (you can also click Join chat).

  • See what the shopper is typing before they send it, live
  • The shopper sees when you're typing
  • Suggest reply (✨): the assistant drafts a reply as your team, using your products, the shopper's order and your saved answers. Edit it, then send. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Saved replies: insert one from the list, or type / in the reply box (for example /ship). {first_name} is filled in with the shopper's name when they shared it.
  • Attach photos or PDFs (up to 3 per message, 5 MB each)

Ending the chat​

Click Hand back to AI when you're done, or Close the conversation. The shopper is then asked to rate the chat (1–5 stars); the rating appears on the conversation and in Aixly impact on your dashboard.

If nobody from the team replies, the assistant takes over again so the shopper is never stuck:

  • Nobody joined within 5 minutes of the hand-off (your team gets a reminder at 3 minutes).
  • Someone joined but hasn't replied or typed for 10 minutes.

The shopper then sees: "Our team couldn't join right now, so the assistant will keep helping. Leave your email or phone if you'd like the team to follow up." The note stays in the conversation history. If you reply afterwards, the chat is live with your team again. Change the wording in Settings → Live chat & handoff.

Replies the shopper missed​

If you reply after the shopper has left the store and they gave their email in the chat, Aixly emails them your reply with a Continue the chat link. The link reopens the same conversation on any device and expires after 14 days. Turn this off with Email shoppers the replies they missed.
