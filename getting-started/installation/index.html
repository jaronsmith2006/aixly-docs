Installation

1) Install the app​

  • Install Aixly from the Shopify App Store and approve the permissions Shopify shows you.
  • The app opens inside Shopify Admin → Apps → Aixly. There's nothing else to connect: the app sets itself up the first time it opens.
When an update needs new permissions

Shopify asks you to approve them the next time you open the app after the update. Until you do, only the features that need them (for example, filing returns in Shopify) stay off. Everything else keeps working.

2) Follow the Setup Guide​

Open Dashboard. The Setup Guide walks you through:

  • AI processing: your store pages and policies are read so the assistant can answer questions about them. This usually takes a minute or two. Products, prices and stock are always read live.
  • Turn on the widget: opens your theme editor
  • Check it's live: confirms the chat button appears on your storefront
  • Try your bot and choose a plan

3) Turn on the chat widget (theme app embed)​

In Shopify admin:

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes
  2. Click Customize
  3. Open App embeds
  4. Enable Aixly AI Chatbot
  5. Click Save

4) Check it's live​

Open your storefront: the chat button should appear (usually bottom right).

If it doesn't, go back to Dashboard and click Refresh status in the Setup Guide. Still nothing? See Troubleshooting → Common issues.
